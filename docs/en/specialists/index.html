The Annaelle team

Professional care with a personal touch

Before the treatment, you’ll meet your specialist, discuss the selected areas, and ask every question that matters to you.

Your specialist confirms the necessary information, explains each stage, and agrees with you on a comfortable way to communicate during the treatment. If anything feels different, you can say so at any time.

Our standard of care

What you can expect from an Annaelle specialist

Shared principles that help make every visit clear and comfortable.

01

Calm communication

Your specialist explains every step in clear language and leaves plenty of time for questions.

02

Attentive to your comfort

During the treatment, your specialist checks how you feel and adjusts based on your feedback.

03

Meticulous treatment

The focus is on precision, privacy, and gentle care of the selected area.

04

Support after your visit

After the treatment, your specialist provides clear guidance on at-home care and your next visit.

Together with your specialist

Four unhurried steps

No uncertainty: every stage is clear in advance and follows a thoughtful sequence.

  1. 01

    Getting acquainted

    We discuss your needs, selected treatment areas, and important information before the procedure.

  2. 02

    Choosing your settings

    Your specialist explains the process and chooses settings tailored to your individual needs.

  3. 03

    Performing the treatment

    You can tell your specialist how you feel at any time.

  4. 04

    Creating your plan

    You receive aftercare guidance and a suggested time frame for your next visit.
