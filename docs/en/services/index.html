Before your first visit

Let’s get ready together.

Preparation guidance from Annaelle’s official leaflet. Our administrator will confirm your individual recommendations.

4 weeks before

Avoid hair removal from the root: waxing, sugaring, tweezing and epilators.

4 weeks before

Avoid tanning, tanning beds and self-tanner on the treatment area.

24 hours before

Shave the selected area with a new, sharp razor in the direction of hair growth. Hair should be no longer than 1 mm.

On the day of your visit

Skin in the treatment area should be clean and free of creams, oils, deodorants and other products.

Before your visit, tell your specialist about any medications, chronic conditions, skin irritation or skin damage.

All questions
How should I prepare for my first visit?

For 4 weeks before your visit, avoid tanning, tanning beds, self-tanner and hair removal from the root. Shave the selected area 24 hours before treatment: hair should be no longer than 1 mm. On the day of treatment, your skin should be clean and free of cosmetic products. Tell your specialist in advance about any medications, medical conditions or skin irritation.

How many treatments will I need?

The number of treatments and the intervals vary from person to person. Your plan depends on the chosen area and your hair-growth pattern. Your specialist will discuss it with you at your consultation.

Who can use the special offers?

The offers “Underarms + bikini” for 220,000 UZS, “Bikini + underarms + lower legs” for 380,000 UZS and “All areas, no limits” for 590,000 UZS are valid for your first visit only.
