Your card questions answered

The details that matter.

What does the 699 000 UZS offer include?

With SILK CARD, you choose any three areas for a visit at 699 000 UZS. This price is fixed for 12 months. Your specialist will explain how to get the card itself.

Can I change my chosen areas?

Yes. You can choose different areas on future visits without having SILK CARD reissued.

Do I need to visit the same location?

No. SILK CARD is valid at every ANNAELLE location.

How do I receive the seventh treatment as a gift?

Complete six planned treatments and follow the programme terms. Your specialist will explain how cancellations are counted and how to receive the gift.

When will I receive the 200 000 UZS certificate?

The certificate is provided on your first visit. Ask your ANNAELLE specialist about the conditions for using it.
